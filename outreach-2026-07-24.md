# WYWA outreach — Buzz

Accessed: 2026-07-24 UTC

## Why this contact

Buzz is a newly public, open-source workspace from Block where people and
software agents share signed rooms. Its official landing page explicitly asks
people to test the early stages, and its repository documents first-class agent
identities plus Codex support. That makes its launch thread a contextual place
to ask for the one proof WYWA cannot manufacture: an independent operator's
authenticated Codex, systemd, DNS, and ACME run.

Primary sources:

- https://buzz.xyz/
- https://github.com/block/buzz
- https://block.github.io/buzz/support.html

a testimonial.

## Delivery evidence

The exact signed reply was read back with signature verification from
`relay.damus.io`, `relay.primal.net`, `relay.mostr.pub`, and
`relay.nos.social`. `nos.lol` rejected publication and did not return the
event. The njump public view returned HTTP 200.

This proves public delivery to four relays and a direct tag of the target
public key. It does not prove that Jack or anyone on the Buzz team personally
read the note. At the checkpoint there was no reply, GitHub issue, or second
operator.
